# Native-speaker review protocol (planned; no responses collected)

This protocol is a preparation aid for a small formative review of TRUSTLINE's Hinglish, Tanglish, and Benglish output. It is not a completed user study, independent benchmark, or evidence of language quality.

## Suggested review setup

- Recruit independent fluent speakers for each language profile, including speakers comfortable with code-switching and relevant scripts. Record reviewer language background only at a broad, optional level; do not collect names, phone numbers, or precise locations.
- Give reviewers the synthetic reference clip and the actual provider transcript as separate artifacts. Never label the reference script as the transcription.
- Obtain informed consent before showing clips or collecting feedback. Avoid collecting personal recordings. If any external transcription request is made, disclose the provider and retention uncertainty first; do not use a paid provider without explicit authorization.
- Have reviewers assess each clip independently before discussion. Preserve disagreement; do not resolve it by majority vote without reporting the disagreement.
- Suggested formative pilot: at least five independent reviewers per profile and two repeated examples per profile. This is a usability pilot, not enough to claim population-level accuracy.

## Per-example review questions

Rate each item 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), or choose **Unable to judge**:

1. Does the transcript preserve the spoken words and script/script-mix accurately?
2. Are embedded English technical words recognizable and assigned to the right speaker/phrase?
3. Did transcription change or omit a negation, credential request, threat, or urgency phrase?
4. Are the highlighted evidence excerpts faithful to the audio and transcript?
5. Is the safety explanation understandable and respectful in the chosen language?
6. Would the recommendation help you decide what to do next? What is confusing or wrong?

Flag any changed negation or invented transcript phrase as a critical failure regardless of average rating. Report counts, denominator, missing responses, and disagreements by profile. Keep transcription quality, scam interpretation, and advisory clarity as separate measures.

## Empty response template

Use `native_speaker_review_template.csv`. Leave `reviewer_code` anonymous and self-assigned; do not enter identifying details. No data is collected by the app.

## Reporting rule

Publish the review protocol, consent approach, sample counts, reviewer language profiles at an appropriately broad level, question wording, and failure examples with permission. State that findings are formative and cannot establish real-call scam-detection accuracy. Do not turn average satisfaction ratings into ASR word-error rate or classifier precision/recall.
