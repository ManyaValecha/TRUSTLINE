# TRUSTLINE Pitch Deck Outline (10 Slides)

1. **The problem** — Voice calls can create urgency, fear, and pressure around money or credentials.
2. **Why current safeguards miss the moment** — Users must interpret context while under pressure; TRUSTLINE is a user-initiated aid, not call interception.
3. **The solution** — “Listen. Understand. Verify. Stay Safe.” Evidence-backed transcript review and concrete next steps.
4. **Product demonstration** — Simulated digital-arrest, legitimate bank-security, and family-emergency examples; label every sample as simulated.
5. **Technical architecture** — React/Vite PWA and FastAPI; optional Gnani transcription/TTS, Cohere context, deterministic rules, and Sarvam translation.
6. **Explainability and responsible AI** — Exact grounded excerpts, typed validation, fallback states, heuristic bands, and no deepfake claim.
7. **Evaluation results** — 220 authored synthetic text records, 35 scenario families. The updated deterministic rules report 98.6% accuracy and 97.5% recall on authored examples; grouped N=44 recall is 87.5%, with three courier/customs misses. Development reaches 100%, indicating template fit. Emphasize this is not independent or real-world validation; AI, hybrid, and full-audio metrics are unavailable.
8. **Privacy and safety** — User-authorized upload, provider data flow, local history controls, share preview, and unresolved server/provider retention.
9. **Impact and roadmap** — Independently evaluate real Hindi/Hinglish calls, add rate limiting and retention/deletion controls, and conduct usability testing.
10. **Closing vision** — Give people time and understandable evidence to verify before acting.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim deployed users, measured real-call accuracy, live family alerts, background call monitoring, police reporting, deepfake detection, complete offline analysis, or verified zero retention. No independent impact or user study has been conducted.
